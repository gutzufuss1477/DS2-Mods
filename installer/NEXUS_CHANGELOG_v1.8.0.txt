Version 1.8.0

- Added Crafting Overhaul 1.3.0 with 120 item unlock choices, optional Free Crafting and equipment durability.
- Added Backpack Classic Overlap 1.1.0 as an alternative to the standard Backpack build. Switching preserves charm settings and prevents simultaneous variant loading. Classic Overlap is not yet tested in game.
- Updated APAS Unified to stable 3.0.0 with optional Episode 2 Early Access. Early Access and Unlock All are off by default; existing explicit choices are kept.
- Includes 23 distinct mods, 24 selectable entries, 312 central settings and 21 INI files.

APAS progression choices can persist in saves. Back up your save before enabling Early Access or Unlock All.
