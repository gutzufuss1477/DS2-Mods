**DS2 Mod Suite**

A standalone manager for **26 Death Stranding 2 mods**, with **377 central settings**. Select your mods, configure them in one place and apply changes with file verification, backups and rollback. All mod files and the ASI Loader are included; the manager works offline.

**New in v1.11.0**

Jump Ramp Unlimited and Beach Jump with Cargo added. Sam Overhaul replaces Sneaky Sam. Crafting 1.7.0 adds ATLAS gear and suppressed weapons; Gloves 1.1.1 fixes vehicle cargo stowing. New options are available in all ten languages.

**Languages:** English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Simplified Chinese, Japanese and Korean.

**Install and use**

1. Close the game. Download manually and extract the complete ZIP.
2. Run `DS2ModManager.exe`, choose your language and check the detected game folder. Select `DS2.exe` manually if needed.
3. Select your mods and click **Apply changes**.
4. Open **Mod Settings** for installed mods, choose **Save settings**, then **Apply changes**. Restart the game.

Standalone application: do not install through Vortex. For updates, extract the newer manager and apply your selection again. To remove a mod, deselect it and apply; customized INIs are retained.

**Included mods**

- **Vehicles & cargo:** Hill Assist & Speed Boost; Pickup, Tri-Cruiser and Floating Carrier Cargo Capacity; Coffin Board Reworked.
- **Equipment & progression:** High-Density Backpack Modules; Climbing Power Gloves Range with optional Combat pickup; Sam Stats Booster; Porter Grade Booster; Extended BT Cord Cutting Range; APAS Memory Costs; Proficiency Bonus Multiplier; Crafting & Equipment Overhaul; Sam Overhaul (replaces Sneaky Sam).
- **Deliveries:** Lost Cargo Likes Booster; No Magellan Evaluation Penalty.
- **Construction & world:** Construction Anywhere; Construction Max Level on Build; Weapons Anywhere; Zipline Range & Speed; Chiral Bandwidth Costs; Infrastructure One Unit; Jump Ramp Unlimited; Beach Jump with Cargo.
- **Scanning & interface:** Improved Odradek Scan; Remote Orders Overlay.

Backpack offers two mutually exclusive variants, giving 27 selectable entries. Versions and individual descriptions are shown in the manager.

**Settings and compatibility**

- One settings menu manages 24 individual INIs for 23 configurable mods. Existing valid choices are preserved; new options are imported from installed INIs before defaults are used. Advanced options are hidden initially. Saving stores the profile; **Apply changes** writes the game INIs.
- **APAS:** Unlock All and Early Access are off by default. Unlocks can persist in saves after disabling them. Back up your save before enabling either option.
- **Crafting:** 132 original item choices plus ATLAS gear and four suppressed weapons. ATLAS/gold and suppressed weapons default on; Free Crafting and durability default off.
- **Backpack:** Classic Overlap is optional and has not been tested in game. Before removal, unequip extra charms, reduce modules to a vanilla-compatible layout, save and close the game.
- Known Sneaky Sam files are replaced automatically. Old standalone ATLAS, suppressed-weapons and Jump Ramp probe ASIs block conflicting installations; move them out first. Unknown legacy files are retained. The ASI Loader remains installed for other plugins.

**Requirements:** Windows x64, .NET Framework 4.8 and the verified Steam `DS2.exe 1.10.89.0`. Other game builds are blocked. The unsigned manager may trigger SmartScreen; use the official download and published checksum.
