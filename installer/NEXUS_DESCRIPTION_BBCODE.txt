[b]DS2 Mod Suite[/b]

A standalone manager for [b]26 Death Stranding 2 mods[/b], with [b]377 central settings[/b]. Select your mods, configure them in one place and apply changes with file verification, backups and rollback. All mod files and the ASI Loader are included; the manager works offline.

[b]New in v1.11.0[/b]

Jump Ramp Unlimited and Beach Jump with Cargo added. Sam Overhaul replaces Sneaky Sam. Crafting 1.7.0 adds ATLAS gear and suppressed weapons; Gloves 1.1.1 fixes vehicle cargo stowing. New options are available in all ten languages.

[b]Languages:[/b] English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Simplified Chinese, Japanese and Korean.

[b]Install and use[/b]

[list=1]
[*]Close the game. Download manually and extract the complete ZIP.
[*]Run DS2ModManager.exe, choose your language and check the detected game folder. Select DS2.exe manually if needed.
[*]Select your mods and click [b]Apply changes[/b].
[*]Open [b]Mod Settings[/b] for installed mods, choose [b]Save settings[/b], then [b]Apply changes[/b]. Restart the game.
[/list]

Standalone application: do not install through Vortex. For updates, extract the newer manager and apply your selection again. To remove a mod, deselect it and apply; customized INIs are retained.

Backpack offers two mutually exclusive variants, giving 27 selectable entries. Versions and individual descriptions are shown in the manager.

[b]Settings and compatibility[/b]

[list]
[*]One settings menu manages 24 individual INIs for 23 configurable mods. Existing valid choices are preserved; new options are imported from installed INIs before defaults are used. Advanced options are hidden initially. Saving stores the profile; [b]Apply changes[/b] writes the game INIs.
[*][b]APAS:[/b] Unlock All and Early Access are off by default. Unlocks can persist in saves after disabling them. Back up your save before enabling either option.
[*][b]Crafting:[/b] 132 original item choices plus ATLAS gear and four suppressed weapons. ATLAS/gold and suppressed weapons default on; Free Crafting and durability default off.
[*][b]Backpack:[/b] Classic Overlap is optional and has not been tested in game. Before removal, unequip extra charms, reduce modules to a vanilla-compatible layout, save and close the game.
[*]Known Sneaky Sam files are replaced automatically. Old standalone ATLAS, suppressed-weapons and Jump Ramp probe ASIs block conflicting installations; move them out first. Unknown legacy files are retained. The ASI Loader remains installed for other plugins.
[/list]

[b]Requirements:[/b] Windows x64, .NET Framework 4.8 and the verified Steam DS2.exe 1.10.89.0. Other game builds are blocked. The unsigned manager may trigger SmartScreen; use the official download and published checksum.
