[size=5][b]Improved Odradek Scan[/b][/size]
Explore with a wider view of the cargo around you. Improved Odradek Scan gives Sam a configurable [b]360-degree scan with a 500 metre default radius[/b] and adds a live distance reading to cargo labels.

[b]Features[/b]
[list]
[*]500 m default scan radius, adjustable through a simple INI.
[*]Horizontal 360-degree scan with the game's progressive wave movement.
[*]Extended visibility for recently scanned eligible cargo, including usable cargo you have carried and dropped.
[*]Distance at the end of the recipient line: [b]recipient - likes - distance[/b]. Updates as you move.
[*]Spent and destroyed items keep their normal nearby visibility without cluttering the extended range.
[*]Native scan expiry and marker limits are retained.
[/list]
[b]Requirements[/b]
DEATH STRANDING 2: ON THE BEACH, [b]Steam PC build 1.10.89.0[/b], Windows x64.

[b]Installation[/b]
[list=1]
[*]Close the game.
[*]Open the game folder via Steam: Library > DS2 > Manage > Browse local files.
[*]Extract [b]ds2_odradek_scan.asi[/b] and [b]ds2_odradek_scan.ini[/b] beside [b]DS2.exe[/b].
[*]Start the game and scan normally.
[/list]
[b]Configuration[/b]
Edit [b]ds2_odradek_scan.ini[/b] while the game is closed and restart after changes.
[code][OdradekScan]
Enabled=1
RangeMeters=500
VisualWaveScale=1
FullCircle=1
FullCircleMode=Sphere
ExtendCargoMarkers=1
DebugLog=0[/code]
[b]RangeMeters[/b] is the radius in metres. The parser accepts 50-1000; [b]500 m is the recommended, gameplay-tested preset[/b]. Keep VisualWaveScale=1 for the tested wave appearance. Set Enabled=0 to disable the mod or DebugLog=1 when troubleshooting.

[b]Range and compatibility notes[/b]
The 500 m preset, cargo detection, wave appearance and distance placement have been tested in game. Higher settings do not guarantee detection at the full distance: earlier 1000 m tests still encountered a game-side first-detection limit around 700 m. Below 200 m, the visible wave may remain larger than the configured cargo display radius.

Only the listed Steam build has been validated. Build/code checks prevent unsupported patches; a game update may require a mod update. Mods that modify the same scan or cargo HUD code may conflict. Do not load older Odradek Scan test builds alongside this release. The mod changes runtime behaviour, not save-file data.

[b]Uninstall[/b]
Close the game and remove this mod's ASI and INI. Keep a shared ASI loader if other mods need it.
