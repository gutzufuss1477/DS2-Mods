[size=4][b]NEW INI OPTIONS[/b][/size]

[b]The three new features are OFF by default[/b] so existing players do not have their gameplay or visuals changed unexpectedly. Enable what you want:

[code]
[Footprints]
HideFootprints=1

[GeneratorRange]
Enabled=1
RangePercent=200

[TimefallShelterRange]
Enabled=1
RangePercent=200
RepairRadiusPercent=215
FixRestPrompt=1
SpatialDiagnostics=0
[/code]

100% means the original radius. Generator and shelter RangePercent accept 100-400; RepairRadiusPercent accepts 100-400. For an 8 m shelter, 215% repair is recommended. FixRestPrompt=0 disables the German text correction. SpatialDiagnostics=1 is for developer debugging only.

The included INI also contains the original settings for cargo hiding, movement, Autodrive and truck weapons.
