1.0.1 - 2026-10-26
- Adds compatibility with Chiral Boots 1.0.1 and its suit-colour/texture correction.
- Uses a versioned peer interface and matching asset contract instead of requiring
  a new hardcoded shoe binary checksum for every compatible shoe runtime update.
- Forwards equipment activation and deactivation so the shoe addon can maintain
  and release its private material overrides while N02 owns the combined visuals.
- Standalone N02 and legacy Chiral Boots 1.0.0 remain supported. N02 appearance,
  menu image, ATLAS stats/recipes and the Crafting main mod are unchanged.
