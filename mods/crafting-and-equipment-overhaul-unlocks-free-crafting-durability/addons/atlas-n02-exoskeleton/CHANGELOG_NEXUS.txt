ATLAS N02 Exoskeleton Addon 1.0.0 - 2026-10-24
- Initial optional ATLAS N02 Exoskeleton Addon 1.0.0 for Crafting & Equipment Overhaul 1.10.1 or a later compatible version.
- Graphite-black ATLAS skeleton with red accents, revised geometry and native-game material handling.
- Matching rendered menu image.
- Single-ASI installation; no addon INI or manually installed data folder.
- Works alone or together with Chiral Boots Addon 1.0.0. ATLAS stats and crafting remain owned by the main mod.
- Removing only this addon restores the main mod skeleton appearance without changing saved item identities.
