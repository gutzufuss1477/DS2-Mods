# Crafting and Equipment Overhaul - Unlocks - Free Crafting - Durability - ATLAS Gear

Customize crafting, durability and early PCC unlocks. Includes ATLAS gear, suppressed and Warrior weapons, and the red Higgs guitar. Optional Chiral ATLAS Boots addon installs with one ASI. Free crafting and durability changes are optional; custom items require the main mod.

[Current downloads and installation instructions on Nexus Mods](https://www.nexusmods.com/deathstranding2onthebeach/mods/86)
