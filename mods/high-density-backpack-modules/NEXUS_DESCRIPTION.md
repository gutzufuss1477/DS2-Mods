# High-Density Backpack Modules - v1.1.0

Equip up to 30 functional backpack modules with automatic placement, cleaner visuals and eight accessory slots.

## What's new

- Uniform one-cell logical placement for modules of different sizes.
- Automatic selection of a free cell when a position is occupied.
- Separate visual packing at native model size: excess models are hidden while their equipment entries remain installed.
- Eight selectable charm slots: two visible and six invisible. Only unlocked, equipped charms contribute to the additional effect checks.
- Automatic persistence of the six extra charm selections.

## Installation / updating

Close the game. Remove previous Backpack ASI versions from your active loader folders and copy `DS2_HighDensityBackpackModules_v1.1.0.asi` beside `DS2.exe`. **Keep only one Backpack ASI installed.** Preserve `DS2_HighDensityBackpackModules.charms.ini` when updating from a test build.

Requires an external compatible 64-bit ASI loader and `DS2.exe v1.10.89.0` (PC / Steam). The ZIP contains one ASI only. The log should show v1.1.0 and `status=PATCH_APPLIED`.

## Charms and saves

The last two accessory rows are visible charms; the first six are invisible slots. Extra slots start empty. Their selections are written automatically when leaving customization and are shared across saves in this installation, filtered by current unlock availability. The two physical charms stay in the native save.

## Limitations

The maximum remains **30 modules**. Native module-specific effect limits still apply. Which models are visible depends on equipment and available visual space.

Clean module rendering was confirmed in gameplay, backpack customization and cargo management, including the reported existing-save load case. Visual packing uses native footprints; unusual model shapes or animations can still clip.

All seven currently unlocked charms were equipped together during testing; eight menu rows pass local checks. Not every real charm effect, restart or save-switch scenario has been measured in game.

## Removal

Before removing the mod, unequip extra charms and reduce the modules to a layout that fits their original footprints. Apply and save, close the game, then remove the ASI.
