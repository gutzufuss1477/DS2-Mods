Crafting & Equipment Overhaul

## 1.7.0 - Suppressed weapons and gold ATLAS defaults

- Integrated four black/red suppressed variants: Assault Rifle [MP] Lv.2, Machine Gun [MP] Lv.2, Shotgun [MP] Lv.2 and Big-Bore Handgun [MP].
- Added red menu images and visible suppressors while retaining the original weapon performance and grenade launchers.
- Added [SuppressedWeapons] switches to the existing INI; FreeCrafting and durability settings also cover the new weapons.
- Nexus defaults now enable ATLAS recipes and GoldSkeletonSkin=1. Set GoldSkeletonSkin=0 for the normal appearance.
- Preserved the 1.6.1 ATLAS gold update and all existing overhaul features in one ASI/INI package.
- Retained custom saved identities, including hidden legacy Machine Pistol 302; the redundant recipe is not published.
- Remove standalone ds2_more_silenced_guns.asi and ds2_overpowered_equipment.asi when upgrading.

# Changelog

## 1.6.1

- Added `GoldSkeletonSkin=0/1` under `[AtlasEquipment]`.
- `0` keeps the normal Boost Skeleton Lv.3 appearance; `1` uses the native gold Boost Skeleton Lv.3 appearance.
- The selected skin applies to both the fabrication preview and the equipped ATLAS Skeleton.
- Gold appearance was confirmed in-game on a non-Deluxe installation; existing INIs without the new key remain compatible and default to the normal skin.

## 1.6.0 - Crafting & Equipment Overhaul

Renamed from Crafting Overhaul.

- Added optional ATLAS Boots and ATLAS Skeleton, combining footwear and Battle/Boost/Bokka effects.
- ATLAS gear is available without story unlocks, with Pizza Baker / Boost Lv.3 looks and hip cargo support.
- ATLAS uses the same INI, FreeCrafting and durability options; its fabrication toggle is off by default.
- Added German/English ATLAS text and retained existing ATLAS item identities for saved equipment.
