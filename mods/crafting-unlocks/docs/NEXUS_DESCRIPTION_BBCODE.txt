[b]Crafting & Equipment Overhaul[/b]
Unlocks, Free Crafting, Durability & ATLAS Gear

Previously Crafting Overhaul. Customize fabrication and equipment in DEATH STRANDING 2: ON THE BEACH using one ASI and INI. Version 1.6.1 adds an optional native gold skin for the ATLAS Skeleton; ATLAS Boots and Skeleton remain optional.

[b]Features[/b]
- 132 individually configurable item unlocks, including equipment, weapons, backpack modules, covers and charms.
- 11 usable enemy-drop weapons, including Ghost Blade, and craftable Omnireflector and Chiral Boots.
- Optional free crafting and a durability multiplier from 1x to 1000x, or Unbreakable.
- Optional ATLAS Boots combining footing, impact absorption, kick strength and quiet steps.
- Optional ATLAS Skeleton combining Battle, Boost and Bokka effects at level 3, including the Bokka double jump and hip cargo.
- Original equipment retains its own values and appearance; normal progression remains available.

[b]ATLAS equipment[/b]
ATLAS Boots use the Pizza Baker appearance, weigh 0.2 kg and have 3400 base durability. ATLAS Skeleton uses the ordinary Boost Skeleton Lv.3 appearance by default, or the native gold Boost Lv.3 appearance with GoldSkeletonSkin=1. It weighs 4.0 kg and has 20000 base durability. Its carrying bonus is up to 180 kg with power or 100 kg without power. Battery use and native jump/input conditions still apply.

ATLAS recipes become available at ordinary fabrication facilities without story or donor-item unlock requirements. Their material costs match Transport Boots and Battle Skeleton Lv.1 unless FreeCrafting is enabled. ATLAS follows the shared durability settings. Names and descriptions are German in the German UI and English otherwise.

[b]Quick options[/b]
[code][CraftingUnlocks]
Enabled=1
DefaultUnlock=1
FreeCrafting=0

[AtlasEquipment]
Enabled=0
GoldSkeletonSkin=0

[Durability]
Enabled=0
Multiplier=2.0
Unbreakable=0[/code]
Set AtlasEquipment.Enabled=1 to enable ATLAS fabrication. Set GoldSkeletonSkin=1 for the native gold ATLAS Skeleton appearance. ATLAS, the gold skin, FreeCrafting and durability changes are off by default. Individual item rules control early unlocks for the 132 original supported items. Restart after changing the INI.

Durability covers supported crafted equipment, nine boot types including ATLAS, and Backpack Cover Lv.1/Lv.2. Cargo, order cargo and cargo containers retain their native durability.

[b]Installation and updates[/b]
Close the game and copy ds2_crafting_unlocks.asi and ds2_crafting_unlocks.ini next to DS2.exe. A compatible 64-bit ASI loader is required separately. Existing ASI/INI filenames remain unchanged. Preserve personal settings when updating; an old INI without an AtlasEquipment section keeps ATLAS fabrication off.

If upgrading from the separate ATLAS test mod, remove ds2_overpowered_equipment.asi. Both items retain their saved identities in this combined mod. AtlasEquipment.Enabled=0 hides their recipes while keeping existing ATLAS gear supported. The global CraftingUnlocks.Enabled switch must remain on for that support. To uninstall the entire mod or downgrade below 1.6.0, use a save made before obtaining ATLAS gear.

[b]Compatibility and testing[/b]
Supported DS2.exe: 1.10.89.0. The mod validates the exact executable and hook signatures. Mods that alter the same code locations can conflict.

The full native/configuration test suite passes. ATLAS fabrication, gameplay, hip cargo and save/load persistence were user-confirmed. The optional gold skin was confirmed in the fabrication preview and on the equipped ATLAS Skeleton on a non-Deluxe installation, including after save/load. Individual shoe effects and combat protection have not all been separately demonstrated. On short jumps, the double-jump hint can appear later than the usable input window.

Heavy Machine Gun [MP], quest-only and DLC-only recipes are not added to fabrication.
