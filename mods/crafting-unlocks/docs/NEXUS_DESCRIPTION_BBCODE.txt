[b]Crafting & Equipment Overhaul[/b]
Unlocks, Free Crafting, Durability, ATLAS Gear & Suppressed Weapons

Customize crafting and equipment through one INI. Version 1.7.0 includes the existing overhaul, the gold ATLAS Skeleton option and four additional suppressed weapons in one package.

[b]Features[/b]
[list]
[*]Early unlocks for 132 supported original items, including weapons, equipment, backpack modules, covers and charms.
[*]Craftable Omnireflector Boots, Chiral Boots and 11 usable enemy-drop weapons, including Ghost Blade.
[*]Optional FreeCrafting, a durability multiplier up to 1000x and Unbreakable mode.
[*]ATLAS Boots combining footing, impact absorption, kick strength and quiet-step bonuses.
[*]ATLAS Skeleton combining Battle, Boost and Bokka effects, including double jump and hip cargo support.
[*]Four new suppressed weapons with black/red models, red crafting-menu images and visible suppressors.
[/list]

[b]New Suppressed Weapons[/b]
[list]
[*]Assault Rifle [MP] Lv.2
[*]Machine Gun [MP] Lv.2
[*]Shotgun [MP] Lv.2
[*]Big-Bore Handgun [MP]
[/list]

These are separate craftable variants. Original weapon damage, fire rate, recoil, projectiles, magazine size, weight and material costs are retained. The new variants also follow the shared FreeCrafting and durability settings.

MG and shotgun use existing suppressed rifle sounds. The assault rifle and shotgun grenade launchers remain unsuppressed. Bullet impacts, explosions and visible attacks can still alert enemies.

[b]ATLAS Gear[/b]
ATLAS Boots use the Pizza Baker Boots appearance and combine multiple footwear effects. ATLAS Skeleton combines Battle, Boost and Bokka effects at level 3, with a carrying bonus of up to 180 kg powered or 100 kg without power. Battery consumption and normal jump conditions still apply.

ATLAS gear and the new weapon variants are available without additional story unlocks once you have access to an ordinary fabrication facility. Names and descriptions are German in the German UI and English otherwise.

[b]Gold ATLAS Skeleton[/b]
The gold Boost Skeleton Lv.3 appearance is enabled by default for ATLAS in the supplied INI, both in the crafting preview and while equipped.

[code][AtlasEquipment]
Enabled=1
GoldSkeletonSkin=1[/code]

Prefer the normal appearance? Set [b]GoldSkeletonSkin=0[/b]. This changes only the appearance; ATLAS keeps its combined effects.

[b]Default Settings and Options[/b]
The supplied INI enables ATLAS recipes and all four red suppressed weapons. FreeCrafting and durability changes remain off by default.

[code][SuppressedWeapons]
Enabled=1
BlackRed=1[/code]

Each new weapon has its own recipe switch in the INI. Set BlackRed=0 to use the base weapon colors and menu images. Set Enabled=0 in the relevant feature section to hide its recipes. Existing custom items remain supported while the global mod switch stays enabled. Restart the game after changing settings.

Normal cargo, order cargo and material containers retain vanilla durability. The Heavy Machine Gun [MP] remains excluded because it lacks a normal cargo/inventory handoff.

[b]Installation and Updates[/b]
[list=1]
[*]Close the game and back up your existing INI if you have customized settings.
[*]Copy ds2_crafting_unlocks.asi and ds2_crafting_unlocks.ini next to DS2.exe. A compatible 64-bit ASI loader is required separately.
[*]Use the new INI for the defaults above, then reapply your personal settings. If keeping an older INI, add the missing options from the new file without duplicating existing keys.
[*]Restart the game.
[/list]

Updating from the standalone test mods? Remove [b]ds2_overpowered_equipment.asi[/b] and [b]ds2_more_silenced_guns.asi[/b] if present. Their features are now included in ds2_crafting_unlocks.asi.

Older INIs without the new options retain their previous behavior; the new ATLAS/weapon defaults only apply when you use or update to the supplied configuration.

[b]Saved Games[/b]
Disabling ATLAS or suppressed-weapon recipes preserves existing custom items while the mod remains enabled. Existing ATLAS and weapon identities are retained, including the old test Machine Pistol; that redundant variant is no longer craftable.

To remove the entire mod or downgrade to a version without these items, use a save from before obtaining custom gear or weapons.

[b]Compatibility[/b]
Requires DS2 version 1.10.89.0. The mod checks the supported executable before applying changes. Local checks for the combined 1.7.0 build passed; its final combined gameplay test is still pending.
