[b]Description[/b]
Crafting Overhaul expands fabrication in DEATH STRANDING 2: ON THE BEACH. It can unlock supported weapons, equipment, backpack modules, covers and charms early while keeping normal progression intact.
Version 1.3.0 also includes optional [b]FreeCrafting[/b], craftable [b]Omnireflector Boots[/b], and configurable durability for supported crafted equipment and boots.
[b]Quick Options[/b]
[code][CraftingUnlocks]
Enabled=1
DefaultUnlock=1
FreeCrafting=0

[Durability]
Enabled=0
Multiplier=2.0
Unbreakable=0[/code]
[b]Durability[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Multiplier=1.0[/b] = vanilla durability
[*][b]Multiplier=2.0[/b] = about 2x item life
[*][b]Unbreakable=1[/b] = no future durability loss for supported items
[/list]
Durability is scoped to supported crafted equipment and the seven supported craftable boots. Normal cargo, order cargo and cargo containers remain vanilla.
[b]Main Features[/b]
[list]
[*]120 individually configurable items/variants
[*]Weapons and equipment available early
[*]Backpack modules, covers and charms available early
[*]Craftable Omnireflector Boots
[*]Optional FreeCrafting
[*]Optional durability multiplier up to 1000x
[*]Optional Unbreakable mode
[*]Normal progression remains functional
[/list]
[b]Installation[/b]
[list=1]
[*]Close the game.
[*]Copy [i]ds2_crafting_unlocks.asi[/i] and [i]ds2_crafting_unlocks.ini[/i] next to [i]DS2.exe[/i].
[*]A compatible 64-bit ASI loader is required separately.
[*]Restart the game after INI changes.
[/list]
[b]Requirements[/b]
Windows x64, supported DS2.exe 1.10.89.0 and a compatible 64-bit ASI loader.
[b]Not Included[/b]
Vehicles, costume customization, backpack patches, Chiral Boots, quest-only objects and protected DLC-only recipes are not added to fabrication.
