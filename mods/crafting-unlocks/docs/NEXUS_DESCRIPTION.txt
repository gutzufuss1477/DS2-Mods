Crafting & Equipment Overhaul
Unlocks, Free Crafting, Durability, ATLAS Gear & Suppressed Weapons

Version 1.7.0 combines the existing overhaul, the 1.6.1 gold ATLAS update and four new red suppressed weapons in one ASI and one INI.

Features
- 132 individually configurable original items, including weapons, equipment, backpack modules, covers and charms.
- 11 usable enemy-drop weapons including Ghost Blade; craftable Omnireflector and Chiral Boots.
- ATLAS Boots combine secure footing, impact absorption, stronger kicks and quiet steps.
- ATLAS Skeleton combines Battle, Boost and Bokka effects at level 3, including double jump and hip cargo.
- Optional shared FreeCrafting, durability multiplier (1x-1000x) and Unbreakable. Normal cargo, order cargo and containers retain vanilla durability.

Four new suppressed weapons
- Assault Rifle [MP] Lv.2
- Machine Gun [MP] Lv.2
- Shotgun [MP] Lv.2
- Big-Bore Handgun [MP]

Each is a separate craftable item with a black/red body, red menu image and visible suppressor. Base damage, fire rate, recoil, projectiles, magazine size and weight stay unchanged. The assault rifle and shotgun grenade launchers remain unsuppressed. MG and shotgun use existing suppressed rifle audio. Impacts, explosions and visible attacks can still alert enemies. The redundant custom Machine Pistol is not craftable.

Gold ATLAS by default
The Nexus INI enables ATLAS gear, its gold skeleton appearance and the four new weapons. Set GoldSkeletonSkin=0 under [AtlasEquipment] to use the normal Boost Lv.3 appearance. This affects both menu preview and equipped skeleton; combined effects are unchanged. FreeCrafting and durability changes remain off by default.

ATLAS Boots use the Pizza Baker appearance, weigh 0.2 kg and have 3400 base durability. ATLAS Skeleton weighs 4.0 kg and has 20000 base durability. Carry bonus: up to 180 kg powered / 100 kg unpowered, not added together. Battery use and native jump conditions apply. Custom recipes have no extra story or donor-item unlock requirements at an accessible ordinary fabrication facility. Names are German in the German UI and English otherwise.

Configuration
Use [AtlasEquipment] Enabled=0 to hide ATLAS recipes, or [SuppressedWeapons] Enabled=0 to hide the new weapon recipes. Existing saved items remain supported while the global [CraftingUnlocks] Enabled=1 stays on. The four weapons also have individual switches. GoldSkeletonSkin=0 disables gold; BlackRed=0 restores base weapon colors. Restart after changes.

Installation and updates
Close the game. Remove standalone ds2_overpowered_equipment.asi and ds2_more_silenced_guns.asi if present. Copy this package's ds2_crafting_unlocks.asi and ds2_crafting_unlocks.ini next to DS2.exe. A 64-bit ASI loader is required separately.

Back up personal INI settings first. Use the new INI for the new defaults, then reapply personal settings. Existing INIs can instead be extended with the new sections/keys shown in README_EN.txt; avoid duplicate keys. Without the new options, older INIs keep their previous opt-in recipe/normal ATLAS skin behavior.

Saved custom identities are retained, including hidden Machine Pistol 302 from the tests. Removing the mod or downgrading removes custom definitions; use a pre-mod save before uninstalling. Heavy Machine Gun [MP] remains excluded because it has no ordinary inventory handoff.

Compatibility and test status
Supports DS2.exe 1.10.89.0 with an exact executable/signature check. The standalone weapons were accepted by the user. ATLAS gold and save/load were confirmed with 1.6.1, including a non-Deluxe installation. The integrated 1.7.0 ASI passes local native, GPU, configuration, crafting, durability and loader tests; all 94 patch sites are checked for overlap. A gameplay check of the combined ASI is still pending. Individual footwear effects, combat protection, full AI hearing behavior and long-session stability are not comprehensively verified.
