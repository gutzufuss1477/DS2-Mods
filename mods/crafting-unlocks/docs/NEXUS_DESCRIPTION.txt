[b]Description[/b]
Crafting Overhaul expands fabrication in DEATH STRANDING 2: ON THE BEACH while keeping normal progression intact.

Version 1.5.0 adds [b]11 enemy-drop weapons[/b] that Sam can normally use, including the [b]Ghost Blade[/b].

[b]Quick Options[/b]
[code][CraftingUnlocks]
Enabled=1
DefaultUnlock=1
FreeCrafting=0

[Durability]
Enabled=0
Multiplier=2.0
Unbreakable=0[/code]

[b]Main Features[/b]
- 132 individually configurable supported items/variants
- 11 additional enemy-drop weapons including Ghost Blade
- Weapons and equipment available early
- Backpack modules, covers and charms available early
- Craftable Omnireflector Boots and Chiral Boots
- Optional FreeCrafting
- Optional durability multiplier up to 1000x
- Optional Unbreakable mode including Backpack Cover Lv.1/Lv.2
- Normal progression remains functional

[b]Enemy-Drop Weapons Added in 1.5.0[/b]
- Assault Rifle [RB] Lv.1 / Lv.2 / Lv.3
- Shotgun [RP] Lv.1 / Lv.2 / Lv.3
- Tranq Grenade Launcher
- Electric Rod
- High-Voltage Rod
- Twin Rod
- Ghost Blade

The Heavy Machine Gun [MP] is intentionally not included. The game accepts the fabrication transaction, but this special weapon has no normal cargo/inventory handoff and therefore is not usable as a regular crafted item.

[b]Durability[/b]
- Multiplier=1.0 = vanilla durability
- Multiplier=2.0 = about 2x item life
- Unbreakable=1 = no future durability loss for supported items

Durability applies to supported crafted equipment, all eight supported craftable boots, and Backpack Cover Lv.1/Lv.2. Normal cargo, order cargo and cargo containers remain vanilla.

[b]Installation[/b]
1. Close the game.
2. Copy ds2_crafting_unlocks.asi and ds2_crafting_unlocks.ini next to DS2.exe.
3. A compatible 64-bit ASI loader is required separately.
4. Restart the game after INI changes.

[b]Requirements[/b]
Windows x64, supported DS2.exe 1.10.89.0 and a compatible 64-bit ASI loader.

[b]Not Included[/b]
Vehicles, costume customization, backpack patches, Heavy Machine Gun [MP], quest-only objects and protected DLC-only recipes are not added to fabrication.
