[center][size=5][b]CLIMBING POWER GLOVES - EXTENDED CARGO PICKUP RANGE[/b][/size][/center]

[center]Pick up cargo from much farther away while keeping both glove tiers separate.[/center]

[hr]

[size=4][b]DESCRIPTION[/b][/size]

Climbing Power Gloves - Extended Cargo Pickup Range lets both tiers of the Climbing Power Gloves pull in cargo from much farther away in DEATH STRANDING 2: ON THE BEACH.

Level 1 and Level 2 use separate configurable ranges. The included profile raises their native 8 m and 10 m values to 30 m and 50 m, preserving a clear advantage for Level 2 while reducing the need to carry a Sticky Gun or Sticky Cannon for ordinary remote cargo pickup.

Version 1.0.0 was tested in game: Level 2 picks up cargo from a long distance, switching to Level 1 immediately reduces the reach, and removing the gloves restores normal close-range pickup.

[hr]

[size=4][b]MAIN FEATURES[/b][/size]

[list]
[*]Separate configurable pickup ranges for Climbing Power Gloves Level 1 and Level 2
[*]Tested default profile: 30 m for Level 1 and 50 m for Level 2
[*]Preserves normal close-range pickup when no gloves are equipped
[*]Does not change other gloves, pickup speed, throw distance, or global pickup
[*]Does not modify Sticky Gun or Sticky Cannon range and behavior
[*]Changes only the verified range field of the two exact glove resources
[*]Hook-free resource resolver with continuous reload reconciliation
[*]Exact executable, item-system, VTable, RTTI, and resource validation
[*]Conflict detection and transactional rollback instead of blind overwrites
[*]Does not edit DS2.exe, game archives, or save files on disk
[/list]

[hr]

[size=4][b]INSTALLATION[/b][/size]

[list=1]
[*]Close DEATH STRANDING 2 completely.
[*]Install a compatible external 64-bit ASI loader. If DS2 already loads other ASI mods correctly, the existing loader can be used.
[*]Extract the downloaded archive.
[*]Copy ds2_climbing_gloves_range.asi and ds2_climbing_gloves_range.ini into the game folder beside DS2.exe.
[*]Fully restart the game.
[/list]

To uninstall, close the game, remove both files, and restart. The mod does not modify save files, DS2.exe, or game archives on disk.

[hr]

[size=4][b]CONFIGURATION[/b][/size]

[b]Enabled[/b]
0 or 1. The included default is 1. Set it to 0 to leave both glove resources unchanged.

[b]Level1RangeMeters[/b]
Accepted range: 8-100 m. Included default: 30 m. Native value: 8 m.

[b]Level2RangeMeters[/b]
Accepted range: 10-100 m. Included default: 50 m. Native value: 10 m. This value cannot be lower than Level1RangeMeters.

[b]DebugLog[/b]
0 or 1. Set it to 1 for additional diagnostics in ds2_climbing_gloves_range.log. Critical errors are always logged.

Setting both ranges to the same number gives both tiers the same reach. Invalid settings fail safely without changing either glove resource. Fully restart the game after changing the INI.

[size=4][b]IMPORTANT NOTES[/b][/size]

This mod extends the gloves' native magnetic cargo pickup. It does not recreate the Sticky Gun or Sticky Cannon targeting, rope, or other special functions. Native visibility, obstruction, target-selection, cargo, and state rules still apply, so some objects may remain unavailable despite being inside the selected distance.

Save/load, area transitions, vehicle pickup, multiple cargo pieces, height differences, and obstacles remain useful regression checks. After a game update, do not assume compatibility: the mod rejects unknown executable builds and may require an updated release.
