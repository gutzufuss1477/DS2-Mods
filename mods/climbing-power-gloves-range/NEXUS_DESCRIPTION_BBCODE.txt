[b]Pick up cargo from much farther away with both Climbing Power Gloves tiers.[/b]

Level 1 and Level 2 use separate configurable ranges. The included profile changes their native 8 m and 10 m reach to [b]30 m[/b] and [b]50 m[/b], keeping Level 2 stronger while reducing the need for a Sticky Gun or Sticky Cannon during normal cargo pickup.

Tested in game: Level 2 has the longest reach, switching to Level 1 reduces it immediately, and removing the gloves restores normal close-range pickup.

[b]Features[/b]
[list]
[*]Separate pickup ranges for Level 1 and Level 2
[*]Configurable from the included English INI
[*]Normal hand pickup remains unchanged without the gloves
[*]No changes to other gloves, pickup speed, throw distance, Sticky Gun, Sticky Cannon, saves, DS2.exe, or game archives
[/list]

[b]Installation[/b]
[list=1]
[*]Close the game and make sure a compatible 64-bit ASI loader is installed.
[*]Copy [b]ds2_climbing_gloves_range.asi[/b] and [b]ds2_climbing_gloves_range.ini[/b] beside DS2.exe.
[*]Fully restart the game.
[/list]
To uninstall, close the game, remove both files, and restart.

[b]INI settings[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Enabled[/b] = 0 or 1 (default: 1)
[*][b]Level1RangeMeters[/b] = 8-100 m (default: 30 m; native: 8 m)
[*][b]Level2RangeMeters[/b] = 10-100 m (default: 50 m; native: 10 m; cannot be lower than Level 1)
[*][b]DebugLog[/b] = 0 or 1 (default: 0)
[/list]
Set both range values equally if both tiers should have the same reach. Invalid values fail safely. Restart the game after editing the INI.

[b]Requirements and notes[/b]
[list]
[*]DEATH STRANDING 2: ON THE BEACH for PC / Steam
[*]Supported version: DS2.exe 1.10.89.0
[*]Compatible external 64-bit ASI loader
[*]The mod extends native glove pickup only; normal visibility, obstruction, cargo, and target-selection rules still apply.
[*]Unknown game builds are rejected and may require an updated mod release.
[/list]
